How AsusuAI works

A fully transparent, AI-controlled pool system inspired by traditional rotating savings groups — but trustless, autonomous, and on the blockchain.

Step by step
01
🔒
Connect your wallet
Install Phantom from phantom.app. Get free devnet SOL from faucet.solana.com. Connect your wallet by clicking the button in the top right.
02
🎫
Choose a pool
Pick a pool tier that suits you. Entry costs range from 0.1 SOL to 2 SOL. Each pool has a fixed number of slots and winners.
03
🎪
Get your ticket
When you join, you get a unique ticket number. Your funds lock immediately in the smart contract vault. Nobody can touch them — not even us.
04
🧠
AI draws winners
Once the pool is full, the AI generates a verifiable random seed on-chain. Anyone can verify it was fair. Winners are selected transparently.
05
💰
Automatic payout
80% of the pool splits equally among winners. 20% goes to the developer treasury. All sent automatically by the contract — no human trigger needed.

Frequently asked questions
Can the dev team steal my funds?
No. Once you join a pool your SOL goes into a smart contract vault. The contract code is the only thing that can move those funds — and it only moves them to winners and the dev treasury after a draw. Nobody has a private key to that vault.
How do I know the winner selection is fair?
The AI uses a Verifiable Random Function (VRF) to generate the winning seed. The seed is published on-chain so anyone can independently verify it wasn't manipulated. You can check every draw on Solana Explorer.
What happens if a pool never fills up?
If a pool doesn't fill by the draw timestamp, a refund mechanism triggers and all participants get their SOL back automatically. This will be implemented before mainnet launch.
Can I join multiple pools?
Yes. You can join as many different pools as you like. You cannot join the same pool twice with the same wallet — this prevents anyone from buying unfair advantages.
When does the pool draw happen?
A draw triggers automatically when either the pool fills all its slots OR the countdown timer reaches zero — whichever comes first. The AI then immediately selects winners and distributes funds.
Is this on mainnet yet?
No. AsusuAI is currently on Solana Devnet for testing only. Use free devnet SOL from faucet.solana.com. Mainnet launch will follow a full security audit.
