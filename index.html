FAQ
Can the dev team steal my funds?
No. Once you join, your SOL goes into a smart contract vault. Only the contract can move it — and only to winners and the dev treasury after a verified draw.
How do I know the winner selection is fair?
The AI uses a Verifiable Random Function (VRF). The seed is published on-chain so anyone can independently verify it. Check every draw on Solana Explorer using the transaction link on your boarding pass.
Can I join multiple pools?
Yes. You can join as many different pools as you like. You cannot join the same pool twice with the same wallet.
Is this on mainnet yet?
No. AsusuAI is currently on Solana Devnet for testing. Mainnet launches after a full security audit.
